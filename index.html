The Sublime

Awe, touched with stillness.

The sublime is the feeling of standing before something too vast to take in at once: a river of stars running across the night, a sky with no edge, a silence that seems to have weight.

It is not quite comfort. It sits closer to wonder, the moment the scale of things turns you small and you keep looking anyway.
